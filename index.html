Frequently Asked Questions

Search below, or expand a question. Answers reflect standard Service Desk guidance.

From any UOB workstation, press Ctrl+Alt+Delete and choose “Change a password”, or use the Self-Service Password portal linked on the intranet home page. New passwords must be at least 12 characters and cannot repeat any of your last 10. If you are already locked out, call the Service Desk hotline to verify your identity and have the lock cleared — IT staff will never ask you to email or read out your password.

Response targets follow the priority you select: Critical within 1 hour, High within 4 hours, Medium by the end of the next business day, and Low within 3 business days. Every ticket is triaged when it arrives, so the priority may be adjusted if the impact is broader or narrower than described. You will get an update at each status change, and you can call the hotline with your UOB-ITSD reference for a progress check at any time.

Install the approved VPN client from the Company Portal on your UOB laptop, then sign in with your network account and approve the multi-factor prompt on your registered device. Once connected you have the same access to internal systems as you would at your desk, with internal traffic routed through the secure tunnel. If your MFA token fails or the client reports a certificate error, raise a Network & VPN ticket and the team will re-provision your profile.

First disconnect any docking station, external drives and USB devices, then hold the power button for 15 seconds and try a clean start on mains power. If there are no lights or the screen stays black, do not attempt to open the device or remove the battery. Log a High priority Hardware ticket with your location and a callback number, and if your work is time-critical ask the hotline about a loan device.

Request software through the Application Catalogue in the Company Portal, which only lists titles that have passed licensing and security review. Choose the title, give a short business justification, and your line manager receives an approval request automatically. Licensed or specialist tools that are not in the catalogue can take several working days while procurement and the security team assess them, so raise the request early.

Do not click any links, open attachments, or reply. Use the “Report Phishing” button in your mail client, then delete the message from your inbox. If you have already clicked a link or entered any details, disconnect from the network and call the Service Desk hotline immediately so your account can be protected. Reporting quickly helps the security team warn other staff.

Raise an Account & Access ticket naming the exact shared drive path or application and the level of access you need. The request is routed to the data or system owner for approval, and access is granted on a least-privilege basis for your current role. Access is reviewed periodically and removed when you change team, so submit a fresh request if your responsibilities change.

Quote your UOB-ITSD-YYYYMMDD-#### reference when you call the hotline or reply to a ticket notification email, and the agent can add notes, attachments or a revised priority. In the live Service Desk, updates you add are visible to the assigned engineer straight away. Note that this training demo does not save tickets — the reference shown on screen is illustrative and will not exist in any real system.
